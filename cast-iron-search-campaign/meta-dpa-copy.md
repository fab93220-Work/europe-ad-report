**Angle:** introducing the newly added cast iron range
**Placements:** Facebook + Instagram feed, Reels, Marketplace (catalogue-driven)

**Specs used** — primary text truncates at **125 characters** behind "See more", headline **40**,
description **30**. Every line below has been length-checked. Catalogue ads inherit product data
from the feed, so `{{product.name}}`, `{{product.current_price}}`, `{{product.price}}` and
`{{product.brand}}` can be dropped into any field and will populate per item.

## Testing notes

- **Run one angle per ad set, not one line per ad set.** Give Advantage+ 3–4 primary texts inside a
  single ad and let it optimise the combination — splitting them across ad sets fragments the
  learning signal for no benefit.
- **Prospecting vs retargeting split:** angles A, B and C for cold; E and F for anyone who's viewed a
  product or added to basket.
- **Angle B is the differentiator.** "Holds heat after the boiler clicks off" is a genuine product
  truth that competitors on price can't copy — if one angle scales, my money's on that one.
- **Seasonality:** search demand for this category peaks Oct–Nov and again in January, and troughs in
  June. The same curve will apply here — front-load the launch budget into autumn.

## ⚠ Claims to verify

Same list as the search campaign — I could not load the site from this environment, so these came
from public sources. Confirm before publishing:

- Free UK delivery — all orders, or over a threshold?
- "Up to 20 year guarantee" — exact term, and does it cover the whole cast iron range?
- "12 colours" — the current stock colour count
- "Hand-finished in Lincolnshire" / "cast the way it's been done for 150 years" — confirm this is
  accurate for the cast iron range specifically. If the castings are imported and only finished in
  the UK, say "hand-finished in Lincolnshire" and drop the 150-years line.
- "Guaranteed for years" / "built to outlast the room" — soften if the warranty is shorter than
  advertised elsewhere.
