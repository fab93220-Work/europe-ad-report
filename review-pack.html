The four answers

What have we learned?
That the profit leak is discounting, not media efficiency. We gave away £55,669 in discount over the period — 13.3% of gross sales, rising to 16.6% in August. Media cost £82,238. Discount is comparable in size to the entire advertising budget, and it has had none of the scrutiny.
Where are we better?
Sales, orders and AOV are all up double digits. The account is no longer an agency black box: brand is protected, PMax can no longer harvest branded conversions, spend has moved out of broad PMax into Search, and every change since 31 July is logged with a before, an after and a reason. Bing cost data is visible instead of £22,860 of revenue with no cost attached.
Where are we still weak?
Contribution after media is 7.3% of net sales and falling — 8.8% in June, 6.1% in July, 6.1% in August. Cast iron is our biggest opportunity and it is currently broken in the feed, on the site and in search at the same time. And 35% of site sessions land on pages that produced no revenue at all.
What are we doing next?
Three things before peak: fix cast iron end to end, put a floor under discounting, and get the account season-ready by the end of September. Detail in Next 90 days.
The one thing to take away

Every one percentage point we take off the discount rate is worth £4,196 over a period like this one — against a total contribution after media of £26,601. Moving discount from 13.3% to 10% would add roughly £13,712, which is more than half the contribution the entire trading period generated. No plausible ROAS improvement comes close to that.

On the ROAS drop, before anyone else raises it

Blended ROAS fell from 5.39x to 4.44x. Two things about that comparison. First, last July the accounts were close to dormant — LY Google spend that month was £2,412 against £18,889 this year — so the 5.39x is the arithmetic of a near-zero denominator, not a standard we were previously hitting. Second, at a daily level the highest-spending third of days still returns 4.44x against 4.57x for the middle third. Efficiency bends slightly as we scale; it does not fall over. That is the evidence that says we can spend into October rather than throttle back.

What the discount data says
